\subsection{Hyperparameter Sensitivity: Step Advantage Weight}

An earlier study (V3, December 2025) examined the effect of the step advantage
weight $\lambda_{\text{step}}$ on validation performance.
Table~\ref{tab:stepw_sensitivity} summarises the results.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \caption{%
    \textbf{Step advantage weight sensitivity} (ALFWorld, V3 study).
    A weight of 0.5 balances episode- and step-level contributions;
    higher weights overfit to step-level variance.
  }
  \label{tab:stepw_sensitivity}
  \vspace{4pt}
  \small
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.15}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lccccc@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Config} & $\lambda_{\text{step}}$
      & \textbf{Train SR (\%)} & \textbf{Val SR (\%)}
      & \textbf{Valid Action (\%)} & \textbf{Avg Steps} \\
    \midrule
    Baseline       & 1.0          & 58.2 & 43.8          & 94.1 & 19.8 \\
    step\_adv 0.5  & \textbf{0.5} & 52.7 & \textbf{62.5} & 92.7 & 21.8 \\
    step\_adv 2.0  & 2.0          & 61.3 & 43.8          & 92.5 & 19.6 \\
    0.5 + 128 val  & 0.5          & 88.7 & \textbf{70.3} & 97.7 & 13.0 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

The optimal weight ($\lambda_{\text{step}} = 0.5$) is preserved in all V10/V11
experiments.
